# Terra Earth: worldwide climate evidence

## What will change
- Rename the app, navigation, page titles, exports, and install identity to **Terra Earth**.
- Add worldwide country and city search, plus clickable locations on the Earth explorer.
- Load available NASA observations for selected coordinates and use the same locations in evidence pages, comparisons, maps, student lessons, favorites, and AI explanations.
- Replace the Bangladesh children’s adventure with a bilingual global story and new scene artwork.
- Update the evidence guide, coverage descriptions, and references for worldwide use while retaining the project’s challenge history.

## Evidence and privacy
- Keep existing verified observations available.
- Show missing records and service failures honestly; never invent climate values or imply a sampled point represents a country average.
- Preserve private account ownership, Google sign-in, and server-verified AI calculations.
- Keep English and Bangla available; additional translations are outside this change.

## Technical approach
- Introduce shared coordinate-based location identifiers and NASA loading through the existing server-function architecture.
- Reuse the current trend tests and annual aggregation rules, with source links and retrieval dates.
- Keep legacy district links and saved records compatible.
- Verify a searched worldwide location through evidence, comparison, map, and learning views, then check the new global story and branding.
