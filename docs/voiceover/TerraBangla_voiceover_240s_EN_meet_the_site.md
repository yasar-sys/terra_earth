**[00:50 – 01:30] — What I remember: sixty-four districts**

Every one of the sixty-four districts — I keep their records. [PAUSE]
Vegetation health from satellites. Land surface temperature.
Air temperature. Rainfall. Sunlight.
Twenty-four years of real NASA measurements, from 2001 to 2024,
in every single district.

And here's what I promise you: I only tell you what the numbers say. [PAUSE]
When a trend changes, I run an honest statistical test —
Mann–Kendall asks, "is this real, or is it just noise?"
Theil–Sen answers, "by how much, every year?"
And I show you a significance badge. If a trend doesn't pass the test,
I don't dress it up. And if I don't have the data,
I simply say so. I never invent a number. Not one.

Step back, and I'll show you how I see Bangladesh — [PAUSE]

Switch me from temperature to rainfall, and watch the monsoon move.

**[02:00 – 02:40] — The detective work we do together**

This is my favourite part — when we investigate together. [PAUSE]
Pick any two districts, or one district and two measurements,

other isn't, I'll say exactly that. No drama. Just truth. [PAUSE]

Or talk to me. In the Evidence Lab, describe a pattern you noticed,
and I'll explain the science behind it — using only the measured evidence.
I can't do your thinking for you. But I can show you my reasoning.
