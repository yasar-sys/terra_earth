# TerraBangla — 240-Second Video Voiceover (English)

**For:** NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 Bangladesh — "Be An Earth System Trend Detective!"
**Team:** MEC TERRA_DETECTORS · **Project:** TerraBangla
**Target length:** 4:00 (240 s) · ~575 words · calm documentary pace (~145 wpm)
**How to use:** Each block has a rough time window. [PAUSE] = beat of silence (~1 s).

---

**[00:00 – 00:18] — Opening**

Every year, our climate tells a story. [PAUSE]
But to read that story, you have to look at the data —
year after year, district after district.
This is TerraBangla — a trend detective for the climate of Bangladesh,
built by team MEC TERRA_DETECTORS.

---

**[00:18 – 00:45] — The globe entry**

It begins with our planet, turning slowly in space. [PAUSE]
Bangladesh glows in amber — one hundred and sixty-four thousand square kilometres,
home to one hundred and seventy million people, and one of the most
climate-sensitive river deltas on Earth.
One click, and the camera dives in.

---

**[00:45 – 01:20] — District trends**

Inside, all sixty-four districts come alive. [PAUSE]
Pick any one — Dhaka, Sylhet, Khulna — and real NASA measurements appear:
vegetation health from satellites,
land surface temperature,
air temperature, rainfall, and sunlight —
twenty-four years of records, from 2001 to 2024.

For every trend, TerraBangla runs honest statistics: [PAUSE]
a Mann–Kendall test asks, "is this really changing, or is it just noise?"
Theil–Sen slope answers, "by how much, every year?"
And a significance badge tells the truth —
only trends that pass the test are called significant. [PAUSE]
If the data isn't there, TerraBangla simply says: data not available.
It never invents a number. Not one.

---

**[01:20 – 01:50] — The heatmap**

Zoom out, and the whole country becomes a living map. [PAUSE]
This heatmap is built from real gridded NASA data —
each cell shading warmer or greener as the years roll by.
Switch from temperature to rainfall, and watch the monsoon move.

---

**[01:50 – 02:25] — Compare & the Evidence Lab**

Now the real detective work begins. [PAUSE]
Choose two districts — or one district, two variables —
and TerraBangla overlays their histories,
with trend lines and confidence bands,
and a plain-English verdict:
one trend may be significant, the other not — and it says so, honestly.
You can export the whole comparison as a PDF report. [PAUSE]
Or ask the AI Evidence Lab: describe a pattern you noticed,
and it explains the science behind it —
using only the computed evidence, never a made-up figure.

---

**[02:25 – 03:05] — Kids' game**

And for the next generation of detectives — [PAUSE]
TerraBangla speaks Bangla and English, side by side.
An animated game shows why the planet is warming:
greenhouse gases, fossil fuels, vanishing forests.
Then it shows what we can do:
plant trees, choose clean energy, waste less. [PAUSE]
A quiz follows — instant feedback, one-line explanations,
and a score that keeps children playing and learning.

---

**[03:05 – 03:30] — Trust & openness**

Every chart carries its provenance: [PAUSE]
which NASA dataset, which source, when it was retrieved.
Everything runs offline-first, from cached satellite records.
The code is open under Apache 2.0,
and every dataset is listed with its source in the README.
Real science, in the open.

TerraBangla is fully keyboard-accessible,
works on the smallest phone screens,
and respects reduced-motion settings.
Behind the scenes, a protected admin panel keeps
announcements, content and community chats safe —
so the community can ask questions and learn together.
